# Five questions for the store manager (final, round 2)

One normal step is about 2.5 ft. Letters and numbers are the blue arrows on `render-before-after.png` (the right-hand picture, "AFTER"). A rough answer is fine, for example "about 7 steps". Questions 2 and 3 matter most: they settle where the back (north) side of the front desk and the cubicle entrance really are.

1. **Front door to the front desk (arrow C).** Stand just inside the front door and walk straight north until your toes touch the pegboard (snack) side of the front sales desk. How many steps? We drew about 7 steps (17 ft). Also: from the arch wall (the wall with the green hard-hat panels, next to the first arch) to the middle of the front door, how many steps? The map has it about 5 steps; the laser scan and the photos say about 3 steps. If it is 3, the desk's middle is about 2 steps to the right (east) of the door's middle.

2. **Size of the desk (arrows 3, 5 and 7).** Along the pegboard side, from one angled corner to the other: how many steps? We drew about 6 (14 ft). From the pegboard side straight across to the back (north) side: how many steps? We drew 4 (10 ft); some photos hint it could be 5. Inside the desk, from the inside of the pegboard-side counter to the inside of the back counter: how many steps? We drew 2. Is there a walk-through gap at both short ends (yes or no)?

3. **East gap and the cubicle entrance (arrows 7 and B).** Stand in the middle of the walk-through gap on the east end of the desk and look straight east. Is the open entrance into the cubicle area (the timber post with the dome camera on its left side) straight ahead, off to the left (north), or off to the right (south), and by how many steps? On the map it is drawn about 1.5 steps to the left. Then walk to that post: does the strap rack start right at the post, and how many steps is it from the post to the north end of the rack? We drew about 5 steps (12 ft).

4. **Where the cubicle wall really is (arrow 7 points the same way).** Stand with your back against the arch wall, level with the front desk, and walk straight east (through the desk's walk-through gaps) to the first grey cubicle panel (not the strap rack). How many steps? The laser scan says about 9 steps (22.5 ft); the map draws the cubicles about 10 steps away. We placed the strap rack on the 9-step line.

5. **Felix's raised floor (arrow F).** Stand in front of Felix's walkway, where the one step is. Does the raised floor start right at the front ends of the two wood counters, with plain floor in front of it (what the photos show), or does it start about 1.5 steps further south, as your first sketch suggests? Answer "at the counters" or "1.5 steps further south". Also, how many steps from the step north to the TV wall? We drew 3 steps (7 ft).
