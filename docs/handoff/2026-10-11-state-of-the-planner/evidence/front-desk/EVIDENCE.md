## 2. How a ring works (ring.js, plan.js drawRing, structure.js buildRing, schema.js, collisions.js)

- x, y = centre. w = size along the item's own x axis (east-west at rot 0), d = along its own y axis. Both are the OUTSIDE size.
- The counter is a band of fixed thickness t = 2.5 ft (schema has no thickness field; `it.t` is not in the schema). Open floor inside = (w - 5) x (d - 5): 9.3 x 4.8 for the front desk (i47: 10.4 x 5.0).
- `cham` applies to the band's CENTRE line, not the outer edge. For a 45 degree cut the outer leg = cham + 0.586 x 1.25 = cham + 0.73 and the inner leg = cham - 0.73. Front desk (cham 1.0): outer cut 1.73, inner 0.27. i47 (cham 1.75): outer 2.48, inner 1.02. NOTE: collisions.js `octagon()` cuts the OUTER box by `cham` itself (1.0 for the front desk, 1.75 for i47), which is a different shape from what plan.js draws (cham + 0.73); this is an existing inconsistency in the app, not changed.
- `open.sides` use the item's OWN sides: N = local +y, S = local -y, E = local +x, W = local -x; `rot` turns them counter-clockwise (rot 90: local E -> world north). The gap is centred on its side (arc length along the centre line, clamped 0.3 - 45 percent of the perimeter), `open.w` wide (default 3.5). For the front desk (rot 0): east gap y 31.55-35.05, west gap same y; outer flat part of each end 6.34 ft, so the stubs beside each gap are 1.42 ft long on the outer face (i47: 5.04 flat, stubs 0.77).
- Outer outline of fd_front_desk (world): south face y 28.4 from x 109.48 to 120.32, north face y 38.2 same x, ends x 107.75 and x 122.05, cut corners 1.73 ft. The python port ringpy.py reproduces ring.js output exactly (checked numerically for fd_front_desk and i47).
- top = colour of the counter top; led (blue strip + the back desk's photo texture) is false for the front desk; h = counter height (3.5; i47 3.4).

### "Same shape as the back desk" check
Same: type ring, shape `oct`, open sides E and W, open.w 3.5, rot 0, 2.5 ft band, counter height about 3.4-3.5, long faces north/south, short ends with the staff gaps. Different (follows the evidence): outer size 14.3 x 9.8 vs 15.4 x 10, and the corner cuts. Photos 1, 2, 4 (outside) put the outer cut at about 1.6 +/-0.3 ft (alpha 1.6, bravo 1.1-1.7); on c3a12961 my overlay puts the real SE vertex at or beyond where cham 1.0 draws it, so a larger cut is not supported on the outside. On the inside (0c5629c8, 9084ccc3) the angled faces look 1.0-1.5 ft per leg, i.e. the real corner sections are thicker than the model's constant 2.5 ft band; one number cannot match both. I kept cham 1.0 (matches the outer outline, which is what the footprint and aisles use) and wrote the limitation into toConfirm. If the owner prefers a look identical to i47, set cham 1.75 (outer 2.5, inner 1.0): the footprint would then be 0.8 ft more cut at each outside corner than the photos show. Alpha's remark that i47's 1.75 may overstate its outer cut is plausible but untested (no back-desk photo in the set): not changed.
