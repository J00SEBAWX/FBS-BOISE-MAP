# Questions for the store manager (8)

The numbers 1 to 11 are the blue arrows on `render-before-after.png`. "A step" means one normal walking step, about 2 1/2 feet. A rough count is enough. My guess is in square brackets, so "yes", "no" or your own number is all I need.

1. **Front doorway and the front sales desk (arrow 10).** Stand in the front doorway where photo 10 was taken. How many steps west to the wall with the hard-hat arches? [about 2] How many steps straight north until you reach the front edge of the front sales desk (the checkout counter)? [7 to 8] How many steps wide is the counter's front, end to end? [about 6]

2. **Felix's desk (arrows 2 and 3).** Stand at the step into Felix's walkway (between the two wood desks). How many steps west to the wall with the GOOD STUFF board? [about 3] How many steps wide is the walkway between the two desks? [1]

3. **Strap rack (arrow 5).** Stand by the big dark timber post at the corner of the grey cubicle panels. Does the strap pegboard start right at that post? [yes] Walking south along it, how many steps until the whole strap rack, including the Maverick knife case, ends? [about 5; the strap board alone about 3]

4. **Cubicle panels (arrows 5 and 8).** Do the grey panels on the west side run unbroken from that timber post south to the north end of the front sales desk, except one gap about 2 steps wide? [yes] Walking east along the panels at the timber post, about how many steps until the cubicle area ends? [7 to 8]

5. **Offices upstairs in the north (arrows 6 and 7).** After you climb the carpeted stair you turn right at the top. Do you come out upstairs over the hall behind the Owners board, on the same floor whose railing carries the 250 YEARS banner? [yes]

6. **Balcony over the coffee counter (arrows 9 and 11).** Stand at the front edge of the coffee counter, in front of the ice machine. How many steps north before you are out from under the balcony? [1 to 2] Does the upstairs floor also carry on west over the front-room side, past the ice machine toward the front door? [not sure]

7. **Popcorn machine, dispatch counter and the open wooden stair (arrow 11).** Stand at the popcorn machine. How many steps east to the near end of the dispatch counter? [1 to 2] How many steps east to the bottom step of the open wooden stair? [5 to 6] From that bottom step, how many steps east to the east wall? [6 to 7]

8. **A second big timber post (arrows 10 and 5).** The big timber post at the cubicle corner holds up the beam under the 250 YEARS railing. Is there another big timber post further east along the same beam, about 7 steps from the first? [not sure]
