# What changed on the map, in plain words

This is a draft for the north-east / east half of the store, built from your 11 photos, the scan and your marks on the screenshots. Nothing has been applied to the live map. The picture `render-before-after.png` shows the map now (left) and the draft (right) at the same scale. Items outlined in orange moved, items outlined in green are new, and a red X on the left marks something that goes away. Every moved or new item carries a note in the app saying what to measure to confirm it.

"Sure" means the photos and the scan agree. "Fairly sure" means they agree on what it is and which way it faces, but the spot could be 2 to 4 feet off. "Not sure" means only the photos show it and it could be 5 feet or more off.

## Sure

1. **Felix's desk moved to where it really is.** The two wood desks with the one-step walkway, the TV and mirror wall and the mini fridge now sit against the arch wall beside the carpeted stair (about x 103 to 117, y 53 to 59). The old map had them at the front, where the checkout counter really stands. The walkway now runs north to south. The two "cedar desk" items were the same desk drawn twice, so they are gone. The "GOOD STUFF corridor wall" is gone too: the board hangs on the arch wall, and no separate wall is there.
2. **The carpeted stair is on the map.** It is inside the walled stair block beside the supply-closet door, with its foot facing east. The supply-closet door that was also drawn at x 150 is the same door, so that copy is gone.
3. **The Owners board moved** from the far east wall to the north wall, between the stair block and the wide doorway, facing south.
4. **The back room is where the photos show it.** The dark plank floor, sink, men's room door and first-aid board are in the north-east back room, behind the Owners board. The wide wooden doorway into it (12 ft) is open now; an old wall that had closed it was cut back. The steel door in its north wall is shown. The "vault" walls, the extra men's room door and the restroom wall in the east are gone, because no photo shows them there.
5. **The big timber post** at the corner of the grey cubicle panels is at the north end, beside the Owners-board corner, not in the middle of the floor.

## Fairly sure

6. **The front sales desk (checkout counter) is on the map.** It was missing. It is an eight-sided counter with openings on the east and west, about 14 ft wide and 10 ft deep, about 19 ft straight north of the front doorway.
7. **The cubicle area is bigger.** The scan shows the panels running all the way north to the post. The block now goes from y 27 to y 60 instead of stopping at y 45.
8. **The strap isle is 12 ft long** (the length you approved), faces west on the cubicle panels, and its north end is at the timber post. Your mark on the live map was a few feet further south, but the live map's cubicle corner was in the wrong place, and every photo shows the strap board starting at the post.
9. **The upper floor over the hall (north)** now starts at y 60 (the railing with the 250 YEARS banner) and runs east from the top of the carpeted stair, not from the old line at y 47.
10. **A second row of white cabinets** east of the ice machine (the scan measured it) is on the map.
