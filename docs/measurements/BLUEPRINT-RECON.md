# Your marked-up blueprint, applied to the store map

*For AJ. FBS Boise store map, 9 Oct 2026. Sources: your photo of the original "Ground Level FLOOR PLAN" with your blue markups, the 3D scan, and your photo survey. This version was checked by a second, independent reviewer. The changes it made are listed at the end.*

All positions are in feet. **x** is measured east from the inside face of the warehouse west wall. **y** is measured north from the inside face of the south wall.

## How it was used

- **Your blue marks count most.** They show the store as it is today. They are hand-drawn, so each one has a tolerance of about 1 to 3 ft.
- **The black linework is the store as first built.** The store has been renovated since, so the drawing was only used to line things up and to back up the scan. Nothing was added on the drawing's word alone.
- **How the photo was lined up.** The photo was stretched onto the map using these walls:
  - the west wall, the grid-C wall (x 50), the arch wall (grid E, x 103), the south wall and the north wall, all measured by the scan;
  - the east wall (x 169). The scan did not reach the east end, so this wall comes from your photo survey and the building's 169 ft length.
  - Those walls line up with the grid lines *by construction*, so they don't prove anything on their own. The independent checks are listed below.
- **How accurate it is.**
  - It is usually within about half a foot.
  - Along the curled top edge, where your hands hold the paper, it can be up to about 1.4 ft out.
  - A second, separate fit was done in a different way. It agreed with the first one to within about half a foot everywhere.
- **East end (grids F, G and H).** These have an extra 1 to 3 ft of doubt.
  - The drawing spaces those grids 23 ft apart.
  - The scan puts the arch wall about 3 ft east of where the drawing's own spacing would put grid E.
  - Until the east wall is taped, positions east of x 125 can't be pinned down any closer.
- **Your grey cover-ups were left alone.** Those areas were not read and are treated as unknown.
