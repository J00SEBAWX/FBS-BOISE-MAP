# FBS Boise Store Map

A private website for the FBS Boise store, in Franklin Building Supply's
colours: the store in 3D and on a floor plan, and a **planner** where the team
can try out where every department should go. It lives at
**https://franklinmap-boise.com**.

## What it is

- **The master map** is the confirmed layout: how the store is (or is meant to
  be) set up today. Everybody can open it; only the admin changes it, and every
  change is saved as a new numbered version, so nothing is ever lost.
- **Your own maps** are your sandbox. Make as many as you like (up to 30), try
  an idea, move things around, and nothing on the master changes. When you
  like what you made, **submit it for review** and the admin can put it on the
  master.
- Three **displays** show whichever map is open:
  - **Plan** (opens first): the floor from above. Click anything to see what it is, how big
    it is and where it is; **Go look at it** walks you over to face it.
  - **3D**: the whole store. Drag to turn around it, right-drag
    (or two fingers) to move, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to focus a
    spot. **Top view** looks straight down with north at the top; **Reset
    view** goes back to the whole store. The 3D scan of the real building is
    drawn as a see-through purple "ghost" so you can check that everything
    lines up.
  - **Walk**: you stand just inside the front door at a customer's eye height
    and look and walk around. The card in the corner says which part of the
    store you are in; tap the small map to walk there.
- **Measure** (Plan and 3D) puts a cursor on the floor that snaps to the
  6-inch grid, to walls and to columns, and shows the distance to the nearest
  wall; click once for A and again for B to measure between two spots.
- **Layers** turns things on and off: the 3D scan (Off / Ghost / Solid),
  structure, fixtures (Shown / Faint / Hidden on the plan), departments,
  ceiling, labels, dimensions, the grid and the scan lines. **Snap** makes
  things click to the grid, walls and each other. The account menu has Theme
  (System / Light / Dark).

If the site was updated before its map database was ready, you will see a
blue note at the top of the map: *"Editing isn't switched on yet. You're
viewing the master map as it stands."* The Plan, 3D, Walk, Layers and Measure
all still work; making maps and editing wait until the admin finishes the
setup (the **Check again** button picks it up without reloading).

## How to use the planner

This is for the store manager. Everything is done on the map itself; there is
nothing to install. It works on a computer, a tablet and a phone.

1. **Make your own map.** Tap the map name at the top, then **New map** (or
   **Start my own plan** on the blue note). It starts as **a blank store**:
   the walls, doors, columns, stairs and the built-in desks, ready for you to
   place departments, aisles and displays. You can pick **the master map** (a
   full copy of today's layout) or **a copy of another map** you can see
   instead. Give it a name. Only you can change it, and it saves by itself
   ("Saved" at the top). Area planning on the master map shows the same empty
   store, read-only, so you can see what a new plan starts from.
2. **Switch to Area planning** (the button with that name at the top, or press **2**). New maps open there.
   This is where you change things. On a phone the buttons are along the
   bottom of the screen: **Add** and **Departments**.
3. **Place every department.** The **Departments** list (on the left on a
   computer, the **Departments** button on a phone) has all ten departments,
   plus any you add with **New category** (name, target size in square feet,
   colour, and the products it holds). Press **Place** next to a department, then tap the
   floor where it goes: it lands at its usual size there. (If you drag on
   the floor instead, the block is exactly the rectangle you drew.) On a phone two fingers still move the map while a department is
   waiting. Drag the block to move it and drag its corners to resize it. (In
   the 3D view, click the floor to drop it.) The panel shows its square feet against the target and offers
   **Size to target**. A block that sits on a wall, a column, the stairs, a
   desk, a doorway or another block turns red and says why; the red
   **warnings** note takes you to the first one.
4. **Move, resize, rotate or delete anything.** Click (or tap) a shelf, rack,
   desk, display or department block, then drag it; the corner handles resize
   it and the round handle on top turns it. **Delete** (the button, or the
   Delete key while the plan or the 3D view is selected, never while you are
   typing in a box) removes it, and the message that appears has an **Undo**; you
   can also press **Ctrl+Z** any time, and **Redo** if you change your mind.
   Walls, doors, columns, floors and stairs are locked so they don't move by
   accident: unlock the **Structure** (in **More**, on a computer and a phone) when
   you really mean to change them.
5. **Find a product.** Type what you stock into **Find a product** (for
   example "M18 battery" or "Diablo blade") at the top of the Departments
   list. It tells you which department that product belongs in and why; tap
   the answer to see that department on the map, or **Place it** if it isn't
   on your map yet.
6. **Compare.** **Comparison** (key 3) puts two layouts side by side, or on top
   of each other, and lists every difference in plain words ("Moved
   “Safety isle” 4'6" north"). By default it compares the master with the map
   you have open.
7. **Ask the Advisor.** **Advisor** (key 4) checks the open map against
   retail-flow rules (open space just inside the front door, aisles wide
   enough, doorways kept clear, related departments next to each other, each
   department close to its target size…), gives each a score and a reason, and
   offers **Show me** (highlights the spot) and **Apply** (makes the change,
   which you can undo). The numbers behind it are assumptions, and the admin
   can change them.
8. **Print, save as a PDF or email it.** **Print / share** (top right; on a phone
   **More** → **Print, PDF or email**) makes one letter-size page of the map as
   it is on screen, with the departments and their square feet: **Download
   PDF**, **Print**, or **Email or share** (on a phone the share menu with the
   PDF attached; on a computer the PDF downloads and an email opens for it).
9. **Submit for review.** When your map is ready, press **Submit for review**
   at the top, say what you changed, and send it. The admin sees exactly what
   differs from the master, and can approve all of it or only some. The button
   then shows where it stands: **Submitted**, **Approved**, **Needs changes**
   or **Declined**, with the admin's note. You can withdraw it or send it
   again; your map stays yours the whole time.

**Current layout** (key 1) shows the master as confirmed, read-only: handy
for checking what is official. For a **blank slate** (just the building's
bones), start a new map from **a blank store**, or press **Hide** under
"Shelves, racks and displays" in the Departments list; both are in Area
planning, so you can start placing departments straight away.

**The guides.** A step-by-step planning guide for the team (PDF, with pictures)
downloads from the **?** controls box and from **More** → **Step-by-step guide
(PDF)**. The admin guide is in the account menu and on the dashboard
(**Admin guide (PDF)**); only the admin can open it.

## How people get in

Nobody can see the map without signing in. Everything (the pages, the 3D
store, the photos, the floor plan) stays locked until they do.

1. **Send them the link** (https://franklinmap-boise.com) **and one access
   code each.** Codes look like `ABCD-EF23`. Each code works **once**.
2. They enter the code, then their **name** and **mobile number**. Then the
   map opens, and they stay signed in on that phone or computer (for up to
   90 days, as long as they come back at least once a month).
3. Only you can see their name and number, in your admin dashboard.

In the dashboard (**https://franklinmap-boise.com/admin/**):

- **Access codes**: there are **5 codes at a time**. Each one shows
  *Available* (with **Copy** and **Copy invite** buttons; the invite is a
  ready-to-send text with the link and the code) or *Used by* someone.
- **Make a new set of 5**: cancels the codes nobody has used yet and makes 5
  new ones. People who are already signed in keep their access.
- **People with access**: everyone who used a code, with their phone number,
  when they last visited and how many maps they have made. **Remove** signs
  that person out right away and **deletes their maps and proposals** (the
  confirmation says how many); they need a new code to come back.
- **Map settings**: **Share everyone's maps** (when on, everyone can open
  everyone's maps, read-only) and **Lock the master map** (when on, nobody can
  change the master, you included). The same two switches are in the account
  menu on the map page.

If someone gets a new phone, just give them a new code. If they use the same
mobile number, they stay one person in your list. The same goes for anyone who
taps **Sign out** (the map asks them first): their code is used up, so they
need a new one to come back.

After you tap **Copy** or **Copy invite**, that code is marked *Copied ·
waiting for them to sign up* (on that phone or computer), so you don't send
the same code to two people.

## Your admin sign-in

Sign in at **https://franklinmap-boise.com/login?admin=1** (or tap
**Admin sign-in** at the bottom of the sign-in page) with your **username and
password**. Change your password any time under **Your account** in the
dashboard (that signs you out on your other devices).

**First time, or forgot your password?** You need a one-time setup code:

1. Ask for one, or make one yourself: in this folder run `npm run setup-code`.
   It prints a code and one command. Run that command once (it adds the code
   to the live database; you need to be logged in to Cloudflare with
   `npx wrangler login`). The code works once and expires after 72 hours.
2. Open **https://franklinmap-boise.com/login?setup=1**, enter the setup
   code, your username and a new password (at least 10 characters).

This keeps your existing admin account; it only sets a new username and
password and signs you out everywhere else.

**Cloudflare Access must be off for this site.** An older version of this page
said to "Protect this Worker behind Access". If you did that, visitors without
a Cloudflare login are stopped before they ever see the sign-in page. To turn
it off, in the Cloudflare dashboard open **Workers & Pages → fbs-boise-map**,
then the **Access** tab, and remove the protection (if Cloudflare instead shows
an Access application for franklinmap-boise.com under **Zero Trust → Access →
Applications**, delete that). The site's own sign-in keeps the map private now.

## Secrets the site needs

The site uses two secrets stored on the Cloudflare Worker (Cloudflare dashboard
→ **Workers & Pages → fbs-boise-map → Settings → Variables and Secrets**):

- `PEPPER` scrambles codes, passwords and phone numbers before they are saved.
- `PHONE_KEY` locks the saved phone numbers (and the unused codes) so only the
  site can read them.

**Never delete or change these two.** If they change, every saved phone number,
password and code stops working. If either one is missing, the site stays
locked and says "The site isn't set up yet."

These old secrets are no longer used and can be deleted: `MESHY_API_KEY`,
`SITE_ACCESS_CODE`, `NTFY_TOPIC`, `NTFY_SERVER` and any `TWILIO_*` ones.

## Who can see and change what

- **Everyone gets their own maps.** Tap the map name in the header, then **New map**: a full copy of the master,
  a blank store (walls, doors, columns, floors and stairs only), or a copy of any map you can see. You can have
  30; rename or delete them from the same menu. Only you can change your maps, and they save by themselves —
  "Saved" in the header means it's on the site, and unsaved edits are kept on your device even offline.
- **The master map is the confirmed layout** the store will actually use. Everyone can open it (it's read-only
  for members); only the admin can change it, and every change makes a new numbered version, so nothing is ever
  lost.
- **Propose your idea.** On a map of your own, press **Submit for review** in the header, say what you changed,
  and send it. The chip then shows where it stands: **Submitted**, **Approved**, **Needs changes** or
  **Declined**, with the admin's note. You can withdraw or re-submit any time; your map stays yours throughout.
- **The admin reviews in the Review mode** (key 5): every proposal is listed with its differences from today's
  master in plain words ("Moved “Safety isle” 4'6" north"). The admin can approve all of it, tick just the rows
  to apply, ask for changes, or decline — always with a note back to you. A plan started from a blank store never had the master's shelves and
  displays: those "removed" rows start unticked (a yellow note says so), so approving keeps them. Approved rows land on the master as a
  new version that records whose map it came from.
- **The master lock**: the admin can lock the master (admin dashboard or the account menu) so nobody — the admin
  included — can change it while the team talks something over. The header shows a **Locked** badge and every
  save answers "The master map is locked."
- **Sharing**: normally you see only your own maps and the master. The admin can turn on "Share everyone's maps
  with the team"; then everyone can open everyone's maps read-only (with a **View only** badge) and copy them.
  The admin always sees all maps.

## How the map is built

1. **The photo survey is the source of truth** for what is in the store: every
   wall, door, column, shelf, rack, desk and display, with its position
   (`x` = feet east of the inside of the west wall, `y` = feet north of the
   inside of the south wall), size (`w` long, `d` deep, `h` tall) and
   rotation (`rot`, degrees).
2. **The 3D scan (Polycam / Apple RoomPlan) measured the building**: wall lines,
   the recessed front wall with its covered porch, the north-east step,
   Von's door and the ceiling heights. Where the scan agrees with the photos it
   confirmed them; where it clearly measured something the photos can't show,
   the master uses the scan and marks the item `src` and `toConfirm`. The
   scanner's furniture guesses ("tables", "chairs", a "bed", a "fridge") and
   its phantom walls (display faces it took for walls) were deleted: they are
   never shown. Every change and every open question is in
   `docs/measurements/` (start with its README).
3. **The owner's marked-up original blueprint** (kept private, not in this
   repository) was lined up with the map and used to confirm walls, doors and
   raised floors the photos could not show: the stair core and old restroom
   walls behind the cedar desk, Von's partition, the 1-step floor, the porch
   piers and the north-east back room. See `docs/measurements/BLUEPRINT-RECON.md`.
4. **The master layout** is the result. Its first version is the file
   `public/data/master.json`, which is put into the database once (see
   "How it gets published"); from then on the live master is the newest
   version in the database and is changed in the app. The 3D store, the plan
   and the walk are all drawn from whichever map is open. The file also
   stays in place as the read-only fallback described above.
5. **What is immutable**: the cleaned scan (`public/data/scan.glb`, and its plan
   lines in `public/data/scan-overlay.json`) is the fixed reference. It is
   drawn as it was measured, can only be shown or hidden (Off / Ghost /
   Solid), and is never selectable or editable. The photos on shelves and desks
   are in `public/tex/`.

## Running it on your own computer

You need [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) 22 or newer. Then, in this folder:

```bash
npm install
cp .dev.vars.example .dev.vars   # throwaway local secrets
npm run db:local                 # creates the local database
npm run -s seed-master > /tmp/master-seed.sql
npx wrangler d1 execute fbs-boise-map --local --file /tmp/master-seed.sql   # puts the master map in it
npm run dev
```

and open http://localhost:8787. (Without the `seed-master` step the local map
shows the "Editing isn't switched on yet" note, because there is no master map
in the local database. On Windows use any folder outside this one for the seed
file instead of `/tmp`.) To sign in as admin locally, run `npm run setup-code`,
run the printed command with `--local` instead of `--remote`, then open
http://localhost:8787/login?setup=1.

- `npm run check` runs a quick self-check of the files.
- `npm test` checks the map's geometry, editing, planning and advisor logic,
  the screens' logic, and then starts private local copies of the site (one
  with a fresh, fully set-up database, one with a database that is still
  missing the newest migration) and checks the sign-in rules, codes, maps,
  proposals, the master lock, removing people and the security settings.
- `npm run test:browser` (optional) drives the real page in Chromium with
  mouse, keyboard and touch (phone screens, the inspector's fields, placing
  departments). It needs Playwright installed globally (`npm i -g playwright`
  and `npx playwright install chromium`), which the site itself doesn't, so
  `npm test` leaves it out and it skips itself when Playwright isn't there.

## How it gets published

The site is a Cloudflare **Worker** called `fbs-boise-map` with a **D1**
database (also called `fbs-boise-map`). Cloudflare Workers Builds watches this
repository: **when a change is merged into the `main` branch, it deploys the
Worker automatically**, and that is what serves franklinmap-boise.com.

Database changes are **not** applied automatically. So when a release comes
with a new file in `migrations/` (the maps release has
`0005_maps.sql`), the person who looks after the Cloudflare account does the
database part **first** and merges **last**. The safe order, spelled out in
`docs/DEPLOY-PHASE2.md`:

1. **Apply the migration to the live database** (before anything is merged;
   it only adds tables, and the current site simply ignores them):
   `npx wrangler d1 migrations apply fbs-boise-map --remote`
2. **Seed the master map** (version 1, from `public/data/master.json`; the
   file is written outside this folder and the command only adds a version if
   there is none yet):
   `npm run -s seed-master > /tmp/master-seed.sql`, then
   `npx wrangler d1 execute fbs-boise-map --remote --file /tmp/master-seed.sql`
3. **Check it with a read-only query** (it should show version 1 and the item
   count `seed-master` printed):
   `npx wrangler d1 execute fbs-boise-map --remote --command "SELECT version, item_count, created_by_name FROM master_versions ORDER BY version DESC LIMIT 1"`
4. **Only then merge the pull request.** The merge publishes the new site.
5. **Look at the live site** (steps in `docs/DEPLOY-PHASE2.md`).

If the merge happens before steps 1 to 3 are finished, nothing breaks: signing
in, the dashboard and the Plan, 3D and Walk views keep working, and everyone
sees the master map as it stands, read-only, under the note *"Editing isn't
switched on yet. You're viewing the master map as it stands."* Making maps and
editing switch on as soon as steps 1 to 3 are done (**Check again** in the
note, or a reload). Nothing has to be published again.

(The access-code feature needs `migrations/0004_access_codes.sql`; the
dashboard's codes don't work until it has been applied.
https://franklinmap-boise.com/api/health shows `"accessCodesTable": true`
once it has.)

## What's in this folder

- `public/` – the website: the map (`index.html`, `css/app.css`, and `js/`:
  `main.js` plus small ES modules in `core/` (geometry, feet and inches, the
  layout model, snapping, editing, departments, collisions, comparison, the
  advisor, autosave; no browser code, so the tests can load them), `shared/`
  (the department catalogue and the rules a map must follow, shared with the
  Worker), `plan2d/` (floor plan and editor), `view3d/` (3D store, scan,
  cameras, picking, department blocks) and `ui/` (panels, menus, dialogs)),
  the data in `data/` (`master.json`, the 3D scan and its plan lines), the
  store photos in `tex/`, three.js and its loaders in `vendor/`, the sign-in
  page (`login.*`), the dashboard (`admin/`) and the FBS design tokens, icons,
  logo, fonts and shared styles (`assets/`, see `docs/DESIGN.md`).
- `src/` – the Worker: it checks every request before anything is served
  (`worker.js`), plus sign-in, codes, the dashboard, maps, the master map and
  proposals (`routes/`, `lib/`, `shared/`).
- `migrations/` – the database tables (`0005_maps.sql` is the maps release).
- `wrangler.jsonc` – the Worker and database settings for Cloudflare.
- `scripts/` – `check.mjs` (`npm run check`), `setup-code.mjs` (`npm run
  setup-code`) and `seed-master.mjs` (`npm run seed-master`).
- `test/` – the automatic checks behind `npm test` (and `test/browser/`, the optional real-browser ones behind `npm run test:browser`).
- `docs/` – the UI design spec (`DESIGN.md`), the API (`API.md`), the steps for
  putting the maps release live (`DEPLOY-PHASE2.md`), the scan measurements
  (`measurements/`) and background notes from earlier work (`handoff/`).
